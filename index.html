DGBiz Idea Pitch Arena 2026 (DIPA2026), organised by Kolej Poly-Tech MARA Ipoh, brings together students, professionals and entrepreneurs from public and private higher education institutions.

Participants develop, structure and present an innovative idea using the Business Model Canvas while strengthening strategic thinking, business development and professional communication skills.

Participation requirements

Rules & Regulations

  1. The competition is open to students from public and private higher education institutions, professionals and entrepreneurs.

  2. Each team shall consist of a maximum of five (5) participants per group.

  3. Each team is required to submit the following:

    • Business Model Canvas (BMC) Poster — The BMC must clearly present the proposed business idea using the nine (9) BMC components.
    • Pitching Video (YouTube Link) — Each team must submit a valid YouTube link to its pitching video.
    • Both items must follow the official templates available on the website.

  1. The pitching video must not exceed five (5) minutes.

  2. Videos exceeding the stipulated duration may be subject to a penalty or disqualification at the discretion of the organiser and/or judging panel.

  3. The presentation must be conducted entirely in either Bahasa Melayu or English.

  4. The proposed product or service must:

    • Be an original creation of the participating team.
    • Address a real-world problem, need or opportunity.
